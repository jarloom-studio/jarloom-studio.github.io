What is a design credit?

One idea.
Two ways to post.

A credit describes your pack's design allowance. One credit gives you one distinct static design, a matching caption and two PNG exports: a square post and a vertical story.

Credits are not a cash wallet or a separate prepaid product. We track the agreed designs within your pack; there is no account balance or automatic subscription billing on this site.

Is this an automatic subscription?

No. The monthly pack renews manually after you agree the next month's scope. This website does not create recurring payments.

What counts as a new design?

A distinct concept or composition uses one design credit. Its square and story adaptations are included; they do not use two extra credits.

How do revisions work?

The trial includes one consolidated revision. The monthly pack includes one consolidated revision for each of its four batches. Send your feedback together so it can be addressed coherently.

When will I get the files?

We confirm a delivery date after reviewing your photos, brand materials and brief. Monthly batch dates are agreed before the pack begins.

What happens to unused designs?

Batch scheduling and any unused design allowance are agreed in the written scope before payment. There is no automatic rollover, cash balance or expiry rule on this site.

Can I pay here yet?

Checkout is being prepared. You can enquire now; we confirm a verified payment route before accepting a paid order.
